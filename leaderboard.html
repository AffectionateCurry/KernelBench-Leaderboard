GeoMean Speedup

Geometric Mean of speedups across all correct kernels vs torch.compile (inductor) baseline. A GeoMean of 1.0× means the model matches torch.compile on average, while >1.0× means it's faster. The leaderboard is ranked by GeoMean by default.

Understanding fastp

fastp measures the fraction of kernels that are both correct and achieve at least p× speedup over torch.compile. For example, fast1 = percentage faster than torch.compile, fast2 = percentage at least 2× faster.

Baseline Types

torch.compile (inductor): PyTorch's optimizing compiler — a strong baseline. torch eager: Standard PyTorch execution — the easier baseline to beat. Kernel detail pages show speedups vs both baselines.
